# Simple Groups of Lie Type

Roger W. Carter. *Simple Groups of Lie Type*. Pure and Applied Mathematics,
volume 28. John Wiley & Sons, London, 1972; Wiley Classics Library Edition,
1989.

The book constructs the Chevalley groups from the simple Lie algebras over
the complex numbers and the twisted groups from them, and develops their
structure: Weyl groups and root systems, the Bruhat decomposition and
BN-pairs, polynomial invariants and exponents, generators, relations and
automorphisms, simplicity and the orders of the finite groups, and the
buildings of the groups. The last chapter surveys the sporadic simple groups.

This is an unofficial editorial edition, typeset anew in Typst. It keeps the
text, the numbering of chapters, sections, statements and equations, the
figures and tables, the bibliography and both indexes of the 1972 book; page
references and index locators refer to the pages of this edition. It includes
the author’s corrections and additions from the 1989 reprint: an account of
the sporadic groups found since 1971 with a list of all twenty-six and their
orders, and a Supplement, Hartley’s Lemma, which refines the proof of
14.4.1(b). The first page added in 1989 was not available; the account begins
with the next complete paragraph, and a footnote in the book says so.

The edition corrects the errors that could be established: misprints, wrong
signs and indices, false statements and gaps in proofs. The 100 corrections
and one bibliographic update are listed with the printed reading and the
reason in a separate PDF, and recorded in [`corrections.json`](corrections.json)
with how each was verified. Comparisons with the literature are in
[`checks/literature.json`](checks/literature.json); a few steps are checked
in Lean ([`checks/`](checks/README.md)).
